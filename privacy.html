Company Time: Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026 · App: Company Time: Break Pay Timer (iPhone) · Developer: John Moore

Short version: Company Time doesn't collect your data. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Your pay settings and break history stay on your iPhone, and in this version the app makes no network requests of its own. The App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

1. What stays on your device

The pay figures you enter (salary or hourly rate, hours per week, weeks per year), your timed sessions, totals, badges, selected job pack and preferences are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's local storage. We never receive them. There's no account, no sign-in and no cloud sync. Deleting the app, or using More → Reset All Data, erases them permanently. We can't recover them for you.

2. What we don't do

  • No analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We don't sell, share or rent any data, because we don't have any.

3. Holiday looks (no network request)

The app's holiday looks (art, colors, short comments, badges and the dates each look appears) are all built into the app. In this version the app makes no network requests of its own: nothing is downloaded for holiday looks, nothing from your device is uploaded, and the app works fully offline. The only connections are those Apple's App Store makes when you buy or restore a job pack (see section 5).

A future update may download a small public file listing only holiday dates (which look appears when). If it does, that file is the same for everyone, and the app will send no account, device identifier, advertising ID, cookie or personal data with the request. We'll update this policy before any version that does so ships.

4. This website and its hosting logs (stated plainly)

This applies only when you visit these web pages (for example this privacy policy or the support page) in a browser. The app itself doesn't contact this website in this version. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like nearly every web server, the hosting provider automatically receives the standard technical details of each page request, including your IP address, the time, the page requested and your browser's user-agent string. It may log them for its own security and operations. For GitHub Pages, GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes (see the GitHub Privacy Statement).

We, the developer, don't access, receive, request or use these logs. We run no analytics, cookies or tracking on the website and don't combine any request data with anything else.

5. In-app purchases

Optional job packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple's Privacy Policy. We receive no payment or personal details, and the app checks your purchases with Apple on the device.

6. Children

Company Time is a comedy app intended for adults in the workforce and isn't directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, we don't knowingly collect data from anyone, children included.

7. Not payroll, not an employer tool

Earnings shown are estimates for entertainment. The app doesn't connect to any employer, payroll or HR system and reports nothing to anyone.

8. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You control all app data on your device (see section 1). Under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or similar laws you can still contact us with any question.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, we'll update the App Store privacy label and this policy before that version ships.
