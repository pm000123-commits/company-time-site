FAQ

What does Company Time do?

Enter what you're paid, start the timer when you step away, and watch what your employer pays you for that break, live and down to the second. Sessions add up into today, week, month, year and lifetime totals, and you earn badges along the way.

How is my rate calculated?

Annual salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year), then divided down to a per-second rate. Hourly pay works the same way. You set the hours and weeks, so it isn't just 40 × 52. It's an estimate for fun, not payroll or tax advice.

What do the BOSS and OOO buttons do?

BOSS (top right) instantly shows a plain employee timesheet. Tap DONE to go back. OOO appears next to it while a session is running and shows an "Out of Order" sign. Your timer keeps running underneath both.

Do I need an account? Is my data uploaded?

No account, ever. Your pay and history stay on your iPhone. There are no analytics or ads. See the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything works offline, including the holiday looks, which are built into the app. This version makes no network requests of its own. Only buying or restoring a job pack goes through Apple's App Store.

What are job packs? How do I restore a purchase?

The Everyday pack is included with no purchase. Job packs (Construction, Office, Nurse/Healthcare and more) are optional one-time purchases that change the jokes, badges and look. To restore on a new iPhone, go to More → Job Packs → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is supported.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store payments. Request a refund through Apple's Report a Problem page.

How do holiday looks work?

Holiday looks (art, colors and a limited-time badge) turn on automatically during their dates and are included for everyone, with no purchase. They're built into the app. To keep the classic look all year, switch off More → Holiday look. Badges you've already earned stay.

I moved to a new phone and my history is gone.

History is stored only on the device, with no cloud sync in this version. Purchases can be restored (see above), but session history can't be transferred.

How do I erase everything?

More → Reset All Data, or delete the app.

Is this connected to my employer?

No. Company Time doesn't connect to any employer, payroll or HR system and reports nothing to anyone. Use common sense and follow your workplace rules. It's a joke app.
